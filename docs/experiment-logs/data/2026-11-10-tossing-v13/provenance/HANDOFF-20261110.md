     observation.
   - The fixes: EES/DP in #775/#776, DP cost estimates in #787.
   - Real robot: unaffected.
   - Rack EES (Sweep3d) was affected; its rerun waits for Josh's go.
2. **Tie-break (Josh): leave Fast Downward's choice.** A human reset ties with dumping at
   2×0.095, and Fast Downward picks the reset.
3. **EES dumps mostly because dumping is its chosen practice target** (80/95). It ties with
   OpenGripper, and a random tiebreak picks; that's why EES seeds vary (93–296 resets).
4. **DP allocates once**: 599 tosses and 1 pick, so it calls a human after almost every toss.
5. **DP-period is degenerate.**
   - A negative learning rate from noisy early tosses made it stop practising the toss.
   - With the floor (#789), pick-first ordering plus pick's growing allocation still
     starves the toss.
   - It ends at 6.8/10 solved.
   - The paper (Vats & Konidaris) is silent on both points.
6. **Sampler collapse.** The paper's sampler is kept; #780 is closed.
7. **Row-30 crash fix** (cube reset when the robot blocks every spot) is in #765. The rerun
   matches the crashed run event-for-event up to that reset.
8. **Standoff** is a ±5 mm band (1.445–1.455 m), because `TossParameterSet` requires
   low < high. Since #769 the classifier ignores it. Making it truly single-valued is a
   small optional PR covering both the sim and the real robot; no rerun is needed.
