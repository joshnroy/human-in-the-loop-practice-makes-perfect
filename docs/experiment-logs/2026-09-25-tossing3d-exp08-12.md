# Tossing3D EXP-08 to EXP-12: exploration ε, practice budget, cycle length, Model B, and choosing λ

**TL;DR.** Seven experiments from 2026-09-25, copied from the live experiment log
(snapshot 2026-09-27 10:30). An HTML copy of the same log is in
[2026-09-25-tossing3d-exp08-12-html/index.html](2026-09-25-tossing3d-exp08-12-html/index.html).
EXP-08a to EXP-11 are **one seed per arm**, so no inference is supported for them: they
are raw numbers.

- **EXP-08a, exploration ε: no ε effect.** Final eval 5/10, 5/10, 4/10, 4/10 for
  ε = 0.5, 0.25, 0.1, 0. Random throws score 0/30 to 4/20, while the classifier's picks
  score 15/23 to 20/43.
- **EXP-08b, 30 cycles instead of 10: supported.** It ends at 8/10 against 5/10 at 10
  cycles. It reaches 10/10 at cycle 19 and scores 9/10 on 6 of the last 12 cycles.
- **EXP-08c, 100 actions per cycle: partly supported.** It has the best area under the
  curve of any 10-cycle run (71/110), but its final eval is 6/10, because eval swings
  between 6 and 9 from one cycle to the next.
- **EXP-09, actions per cycle: final eval does not rise steadily with cycle length.**
  Final eval is 5, 7, 4, 4 and 6 of 10 at 20, 30, 40, 50 and 100 actions. The 80-action
  run crashed when a pick dragged the bin.
- **EXP-10 and EXP-11, Model B: supported.** At λ = 0, Model B tracks Model A arm for arm.
- **EXP-12, choosing λ.** Model A falls off a cliff as λ rises, while Model B degrades
  smoothly. **λ = 3e-6 became the canonical practice-cost weight** (decision
  2026-09-25).
- **Read every result here with three caveats.** The code in force for all seven
  experiments forced practice onto the robot's side of the barrier (#366; reversed from
  EXP-13). Its search also dropped GraspClear after an imagined toss success (fixed in
  #375). And EXP-12's second, "automatic" reset skill was a relabelled human reset
  (disabled in #376).

## Background

This log continues the Tossing3D practice series. EXP-06 and EXP-07 are recorded in PR
#371, which sits on a sibling branch, so they are referred to here by name rather than
linked. The robot practises the `Tossing3D` KINDER task: it picks a cube and tosses it
over a barrier into a bin. Eval is 10 fixed tasks, all with the bin on the far side.
Between eval checkpoints, a belief-space expectimax planner decides what to practise,
when to stop, and when to pay for a human reset (cost 5). It trades each skill's
forecast competence gain against a linear practice cost weighted by λ. Two competence
models are compared:

- **Model A** (`global_curve`) is an exponential learning curve over a φ grid.
- **Model B** (`local_trend`, ρ = 0.9) is a latent level plus a drifting trend.

After EXP-07, the planner ran notebook-matched inference (#367) and updated toss
competence from non-ε throws only (#368's `non_epsilon`, chosen in EXP-07b). EXP-08 to
EXP-11 then swept the protocol knobs that had never been tested (ε, cycles, actions per
cycle) and repeated them on Model B. EXP-12 picked the canonical λ. Its runs also
validated "fix (b)" (#372): toss feasibility is now computed from the bin's *live* pose,
and an infeasible candidate is rejected rather than raising
`NoFeasibleTossDirectionError`. That fix exists because the EXP-09 and EXP-11 80-action
runs had crashed that way.

**Staleness notes.** These are also carried in the live log and copied per experiment
below.

- **Forced same-side practice.** EXP-05 to EXP-12 ran with practice resets forced onto the
  robot side (#366). Josh reversed this on 2026-09-25 at about 21:40: the planner must
  choose. Every number here is conditional on forced same-side practice.
- **GraspClear bias.** In EXP-05 to EXP-12, after an imagined successful toss the search
  dropped GraspClear, so it under-valued the pick that follows. #375 fixed this; it
  keeps GraspClear unchanged.
- **EXP-12's second reset skill** (`non_human_reset`) was executed by the human at cost 5.
  It was therefore a human intervention under another label, and #376 disabled it.
- **One near-tie decision can flip a single-seed ±1 difference.** EXP-07c and EXP-07d turned
  out to be action-for-action the same run, which departed from EXP-07a at one near-tie
  STOP. The same caution applies to single-seed ±1-task differences in EXP-08 to EXP-11.

### EXP-12: Choosing λ — practice-cost weight sweep on both competence models (also validates fix (b))

*2026-09-25.* HTML page: [exp-12.html](2026-09-25-tossing3d-exp08-12-html/exp-12.html). Results sub-page: [exp12-lambda.html](2026-09-25-tossing3d-exp08-12-html/exp12-lambda.html).

- **Practice side.** Robot side only — FORCED by code, not chosen by the planner. From #366 (first commit 57196f0a, 2026-09-25 00:31; stack commits 47c30231 / fe73dc1c / 8bb9a337) practice resets could only put the bin on the robot side and every practice scene was rebuilt with the bin on the robot side. Josh reversed this on 2026-09-25 ~21:40 (the planner must choose); results here are conditional on forced same-side practice.
- **Question.** Which practice-cost weight λ should be canonical: the largest that saves practice without costing eval?
- **Hypothesis.** Pre-registered. As λ grows the robot stops practising earlier, cutting practice cost (1 per action, 5 per human reset) and eventually eval. Model A stops sooner than Model B at the same λ, because its learning-rate estimate collapses after the first successes (EXP-05/06). Decision rule, fixed before the runs: per model, the canonical λ is the largest one whose final eval is within 1 task of λ = 0's; if the two models disagree, both go to Josh.
- **Results TL;DR.** Model A has a cliff, Model B degrades smoothly (seeds 0–2; n = 3 is too few for firm inference). Mean of the last 5 evals over 3 seeds, paired against λ = 0 on the same seeds: Model A 8.8 at λ = 0; 8.3 (1e-6, −0.5, p = 0.49), 8.1 (3e-6, −0.7, p = 0.36), 7.1 (1e-5, −1.7, p = 0.20), 7.5 (3e-5, −1.3, p = 0.044); every λ ≥ 1e-4 quits before its first scoring throw (0.2–0.6, seed 0 only). Practice cost 1542 → 1114 / 982 / 743 / 555. Model B 8.9 at λ = 0; 9.0 (1e-5, +0.1, p = 0.18), 8.6 (1e-4, −0.3, p = 0.60), 7.9 (3e-4, −0.9, p = 0.47), 6.7 (1e-3, −2.2, p = 0.003), 4.3 (3e-3, −4.5, p = 0.037). Practice cost 1523 → 1122 / 1144 / 1014 / 378 / 100. Rule "largest λ within 1 task of λ = 0": pre-registered seed-0 final eval gives Model B 3e-4; the 3-seed last-5 mean gives Model A 3e-6 and Model B 3e-4. A single λ that passes for both is 3e-6 (Model B untested there but bracketed by 0 and 1e-5, both ~9.0). Fix (b): 0 NoFeasibleTossDirectionError crashes in 37 runs, but its rejection path never fired, so it is untested on a moved bin. One run (Model B 1e-5 seed 1) crashed at cycle 45 with BinPlacementViolationError: the reset could not place the bin because the robot stood in the reset region.
- **Validation.** These are also the validation runs for fix (b) (toss feasibility from the bin's live pose; infeasible candidates rejected instead of crashing): none of the 10 runs may raise NoFeasibleTossDirectionError. Each run's count of rejected no-feasible-direction candidates and of bin moves during practice is reported. The runs are stopped early if either check fails in the first cycles.
- **Arms.** λ ∈ {0, 1e-4, 3e-4, 1e-3, 3e-3} × {Model A (global_curve), Model B (local_trend, ρ = 0.9)}, plus Model A at λ ∈ {1e-6, 3e-6, 1e-5, 3e-5} (added 16:50 after every Model A λ ≥ 1e-4 arm quit practising in cycles 2–4 with 0 scoring throws — testing whether a smaller cost only delays the quit); grid engine, 50 cycles × 20 actions, ε = 0.5, human reset cost 5, non_epsilon evidence, seed 0 only (one run per arm: no inference supported). Code: fix (b) on #370.
- **Status.** Done (21:00) — 36/37 runs finished, 1 crashed (bin-placement bug; since fixed in #374).
- **Decisions after this experiment.**
  - 2026-09-25: Practice starts with the bin on the far side, and the planner chooses whether to reset and to which side. Nothing is forced: the planner must choose intelligently, not be made to make the right decision. (Reverses #366's forced same-side practice.)
  - 2026-09-25: Canonical practice-cost weight λ = 3e-6 for all methods (from EXP-12).

### EXP-09: Practice cycle length — 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 80 / 100 actions per cycle at ε = 0.3

*2026-09-25.* HTML page: [exp-09.html](2026-09-25-tossing3d-exp08-12-html/exp-09.html). Results sub-page: [exp09-cycle-length.html](2026-09-25-tossing3d-exp08-12-html/exp09-cycle-length.html).

- **Question.** How does eval scale with the number of practice actions per cycle, holding 10 cycles (10 classifier refits) and ε fixed?
- **Hypothesis.** Pre-registered. Toss learning is success-starved, so more actions per cycle reach the first success sooner and give the classifier more successes per refit: eval improves with cycle length, with diminishing returns once each cycle already yields several successes (expected knee around 40–50 actions).

What does change: every length ≥ 30 gets its first practice success in cycle 1 (20 actions: cycle 5), and area under the curve grows with length (20/110 at 20 actions, 45/110 at 30 and 50, 71/110 at 100). Eval swings 3–4 tasks between consecutive cycles, which swamps the final-cycle comparison.
- **Code note.** Launched on d7b7ba30, before EXP-07d's change (pick/open/reset counts stay at 0). EXP-07d found that makes essentially no behavioural difference.
- **Arms.** λ = 0, ε = 0.3, 10 cycles, --max-steps-per-interaction ∈ {20, 30, 40, 50, 80}, seed 0; the 100-action point is EXP-08c's run (reused). Model A + grid, notebook-matched code, evidence non-ε. One seed per arm: raw numbers only, no inference.

### EXP-08a: Exploration ε — 0.5 vs 0.25 vs 0.1 vs 0 at 10 cycles

*2026-09-25.* HTML page: [exp-08a.html](2026-09-25-tossing3d-exp08-12-html/exp-08a.html). Results sub-page: [exp08-eps-budget.html](2026-09-25-tossing3d-exp08-12-html/exp08-eps-budget.html).

- **Question.** Is ε = 0.5 (the paper's fixed EES value, never annealed) too high for our wide proposal, where only ~1 in 10 random throws score?
- **Hypothesis.** Pre-registered. Lowering ε gives the classifier more successes per cycle and so improves eval, best around ε = 0.1–0.25; ε = 0 risks locking into its own biases (e.g. under-throwing). Competing null: no eval change, because eval already uses the classifier's pick (the surrogate replay predicted ~0).
- **Results TL;DR.** No ε effect (one seed, no inference): final 5, 5, 4, 4 of 10 for ε = 0.5, 0.25, 0.1, 0. ε does fire (0.5: 20 random throws, 4 scored; 0.25: 7, 0 scored; 0.1: 3, 0 scored), but random throws score 0/30 to 4/20 while the classifier's picks score 15/23 to 20/43, so ε mostly trades good throws for bad ones. All four arms are identical until the first success (cycle 5); ε = 0.1 and ε = 0 give the same eval every cycle despite ε = 0.1's three random throws.
- **Code note.** Launched on d7b7ba30, before EXP-07d's change (pick/open/reset counts stay at 0). EXP-07d found that makes essentially no behavioural difference.
- **Arms.** ε ∈ {0.5, 0.25, 0.1, 0}, λ = 0, 20 actions × 10 cycles, seed 0, Model A + grid, notebook-matched code, evidence non-ε. One seed per arm: raw numbers only, no inference.
