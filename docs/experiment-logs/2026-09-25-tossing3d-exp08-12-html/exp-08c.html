EXP-08c2026-09-25

More actions per cycle at lower ε — 100 actions × 10 cycles, ε = 0.3

Practice sideRobot side only — FORCED by code, not chosen by the planner. From #366 (first commit 57196f0a, 2026-09-25 00:31; stack commits 47c30231 / fe73dc1c / 8bb9a337) practice resets could only put the bin on the robot side and every practice scene was rebuilt with the bin on the robot side. Josh reversed this on 2026-09-25 ~21:40 (the planner must choose); results here are conditional on forced same-side practice.
QuestionDoes a much bigger per-cycle budget (with the same 10 classifier refits) beat more cycles?
HypothesisPre-registered. The largest budget (~1,000 actions) collects the most successes and scores best. If it does not beat 30 cycles × 20 actions (~600 actions, 30 refits), refitting often matters more than raw throw count. (Confounded with ε = 0.3 by design; compare against EXP-08a's ε curve.)
Results TL;DRPartly supported (one seed, no inference): 100 actions per cycle gets the first practice success in cycle 1 (vs cycle 5 at 20 actions) and the best area under the curve of any 10-cycle run (71/110 vs 20–28/110), peaking at 9/10 on cycles 4, 5 and 9. But final eval is only 6/10 — eval swings 6 ↔ 9 between cycles.
Code noteLaunched on d7b7ba30, before EXP-07d's change (pick/open/reset counts stay at 0). EXP-07d found that makes essentially no behavioural difference.
Armsε = 0.3, λ = 0, --max-steps-per-interaction 100, 10 cycles, seed 0, Model A + grid, notebook-matched code, evidence non-ε. One seed: no inference.
